# Refund and Return Policy

This policy explains how returns, refunds and exchanges work at Lumen & Pine. It applies to all orders placed on our website.

## Return window

You can return most items within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund of the item price. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and include all accessories and manuals.

Items that have been used outdoors (for example, a tent that has been pitched at a campsite) can still be returned within the 30-day window if they are clean and undamaged, but they are subject to the restocking fee below.

## Damaged or defective items

If an item arrives damaged or doesn't work, contact support within **7 days of delivery** with:

1. Your order number.
2. Photos of the damage, including the outer shipping box.
3. A short description of the problem.

For damaged or defective items we offer your choice of a **free replacement or a full refund**, including the original shipping cost. We email you a prepaid return label; for items under $25 we may ask you to keep or recycle the item instead of returning it.

Damage reported after 7 days is handled as a warranty claim (see the product manual for warranty terms) rather than a refund.

## Non-returnable items

The following items can't be returned unless they arrived damaged or defective:

- Items marked **Final Sale** on the product page.
- Gift cards.
- Opened consumables, such as fuel canisters, food, and coffee.
- Personalized or engraved items.
- Earbud ear tips, for hygiene reasons (the earbuds themselves can be returned).

## Restocking fee

A **15% restocking fee** applies to:

- Opened electronics (such as wireless earbuds and electric kettles).
- Outdoor gear that shows signs of use.

There is no restocking fee for unopened items, or for items that arrived damaged, defective, or wrong.

## Return shipping

- **Damaged, defective, or wrong items:** we pay return shipping.
- **All other returns:** a $6.95 return shipping fee is deducted from your refund if you use our prepaid label. You can also ship the item back yourself with any tracked carrier at your own cost.

## How to start a return

1. Sign in and go to **Orders**, then select **Start a return** next to the item. Guest shoppers can contact support with their order number and email address.
2. Print the return label and pack the item securely in its original packaging.
3. Drop the package off with the carrier within **14 days** of starting the return. Return labels expire after 14 days.

## Refund timing

We inspect returns within **3 business days** of receiving them. Once approved, the refund is issued to your **original payment method**. It usually appears within **5–10 business days**, depending on your bank. Original shipping charges are refunded only for damaged, defective, or wrong items.

If you paid with a gift card, the refund goes back to a gift card.

## Store credit option

Instead of a refund, you can choose store credit worth **110% of the refund amount**. Store credit is issued as a gift card as soon as the return is approved and never expires.

## Exchanges

We don't process direct exchanges. To get a different size or color, return the original item for a refund and place a new order. If the item is damaged or defective, we can send a replacement of the same item at no cost.

## Refunds over $500

Refunds over $500 must be approved by a support supervisor. This can add up to 2 business days to the refund timing.

## Late returns

Returns received more than 30 days after delivery are not eligible for a refund. Within 31–60 days, we may offer store credit at our discretion, minus the restocking fee.
