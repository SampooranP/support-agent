# Drift Wireless Earbuds: User Manual

Model: DR-200. The Drift earbuds are true wireless earbuds with active noise cancellation (ANC), sweat resistance, and a pocket-size charging case.

## Specifications

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Bluetooth | 5.3, range up to 33 ft (10 m) |
| Codecs | SBC, AAC |
| Battery (earbuds) | Up to 8 hours with ANC off, 6 hours with ANC on |
| Battery (with case) | Up to 32 hours total |
| Fast charge | 10 minutes of charging gives 1.5 hours of playback |
| Charging | USB-C, and Qi wireless charging on the case |
| Water resistance | IPX5 (sweat and rain; not for swimming) |
| Ear tips | Small, medium (pre-installed), and large |

## What's in the box

- Left and right earbuds
- Charging case
- USB-C charging cable
- Ear tips in 3 sizes
- Quick start guide

## Pairing for the first time

1. Open the charging case with the earbuds inside. The case light flashes white, which means the earbuds are ready to pair.
2. On your phone or computer, open the Bluetooth settings and select **Drift DR-200**.
3. When the case light turns solid white, the earbuds are connected.

If the light doesn't flash white, press and hold the button on the back of the case for 3 seconds.

## Touch controls

| Action | Left earbud | Right earbud |
|---|---|---|
| Tap once | Play or pause | Play or pause |
| Tap twice | Previous track | Next track |
| Press and hold (2 seconds) | Switch between ANC, Transparency, and Off | Voice assistant |
| Tap three times | Volume down | Volume up |

To answer or end a call, tap either earbud once.

## Charging

Place the earbuds in the case to charge them. The case light shows the battery level when you open the lid: green means more than 50%, amber means 20–50%, and red means less than 20%. A fully drained case takes about 2 hours to charge with a cable, or 3.5 hours on a wireless charger.

## Resetting the earbuds

Reset the earbuds if they won't connect, only one earbud plays, or the sound is out of sync.

1. Remove **Drift DR-200** from your device's Bluetooth list ("Forget this device").
2. Put both earbuds in the case and keep the lid open.
3. Press and hold the case button for **15 seconds**, until the light flashes amber 3 times and then flashes white.
4. Pair the earbuds again as described above.

## Troubleshooting

**Only one earbud plays.** Put both earbuds back in the case for 10 seconds, then take them out again. If that doesn't work, reset the earbuds.

**Audio cuts out.** Stay within 33 ft (10 m) of your device, with no walls in between. Turn off Bluetooth on other nearby devices the earbuds have paired with.

**Earbuds don't charge.** Clean the charging contacts on the earbuds and inside the case with a dry cotton swab. Make sure the ear tips aren't stopping the earbuds from sitting fully in the case.

**Noise cancellation is weak.** ANC works best with a good seal. Try a larger ear tip size.

## Care

- Wipe the earbuds and case with a dry, soft cloth. Don't use liquids or rinse them under water.
- Dry the earbuds before putting them in the case after a workout.
- Replace ear tips every 6 months. Replacement ear tips are sold separately and can't be returned once opened.

## Warranty

The Drift earbuds have a **1-year limited warranty** from the date of delivery that covers defects in materials and workmanship, including battery failure (battery capacity below 60% of the original). It doesn't cover lost earbuds, water damage beyond the IPX5 rating, or physical damage. A single lost earbud or a lost case can be replaced for $39 through support.
