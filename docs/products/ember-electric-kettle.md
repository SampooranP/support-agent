# Ember Gooseneck Electric Kettle: User Manual

Model: EK-900. The Ember is a 0.9-liter variable-temperature electric kettle with a gooseneck spout for pour-over coffee and tea.

## Specifications

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Capacity | 0.9 L (30 oz); minimum fill 0.3 L |
| Power | 1200 W, 120 V, 60 Hz (US and Canada only) |
| Temperature range | 104–212 °F (40–100 °C), in 1-degree steps |
| Hold function | Keeps water at the set temperature for up to 60 minutes |
| Material | 304 stainless steel body, BPA-free handle |
| Cord length | 30 in (76 cm) |

## Safety warnings

- Use only on a flat, dry surface, and only with the included base.
- Never fill above the MAX line. Overfilled water can spray out when it boils.
- Don't immerse the kettle or base in water, and don't put either in the dishwasher.
- The kettle body gets hot. Hold it only by the handle.
- Unplug the base when not in use and before cleaning.
- Don't operate the kettle empty. If it's switched on dry, the kettle shuts off automatically; let it cool for 15 minutes before refilling.

## Before first use

Fill the kettle to the MAX line, boil the water, and discard it. Repeat twice to remove any manufacturing residue.

## Using the kettle

1. Fill the kettle with water between the MIN and MAX lines and place it on the base.
2. Press the **Power** button. The display shows the current water temperature.
3. Turn the dial to set the target temperature. Press the dial to choose a preset: 160 °F (green tea), 185 °F (oolong), 200 °F (pour-over coffee), or 212 °F (boil).
4. The kettle beeps once when it reaches the target temperature.
5. To keep the water warm, press **Hold** before or after heating. The hold light turns on, and the kettle holds the temperature for up to 60 minutes.

Lifting the kettle off the base pauses heating. Put it back within 2 minutes and heating resumes; after 2 minutes, the base switches off.

## Cleaning and descaling

- Wipe the outside with a damp cloth. Don't use abrasive pads.
- **Descale once a month** (more often with hard water): fill the kettle with equal parts water and white vinegar to the MAX line, heat to 212 °F, let it sit for 30 minutes, then empty it and rinse 3 times with fresh water.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Display shows **E1** | The kettle was switched on empty or with too little water. Let it cool for 15 minutes, add water above the MIN line, and try again. |
| Display shows **E2** | Temperature sensor error. Unplug the base for 1 minute, then plug it back in. If E2 stays, contact support. |
| Kettle doesn't turn on | Check that the kettle sits flat on the base and the outlet works. Try another outlet. |
| Water tastes metallic or has white flakes | Mineral buildup. Descale the kettle as described above. |
| Kettle beeps but water isn't hot enough | Check that the target temperature is set correctly. If readings are off by more than 5 °F, contact support. |
