# Shipping Policy

This policy covers where we ship, how long delivery takes, what it costs, and what to do if a package is late, lost, or damaged.

## Where we ship

We ship to all 50 US states, Washington D.C., US territories, APO/FPO addresses, and Canada. We don't ship to other countries yet.

## Processing time

Orders placed before **1:00 pm Eastern Time** on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed after 1:00 pm, on weekends, or on US holidays ship the next business day. Pre-order items ship on the date shown on the product page.

## Shipping options and costs (United States)

| Method | Delivery time after shipping | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 5–7 business days | Free on orders of $50 or more; otherwise $5.99 |
| Expedited | 2–3 business days | $12.99 |
| Overnight | 1 business day | $24.99 |

Overnight orders must be placed before 1:00 pm Eastern Time to ship the same day. Overnight and Expedited aren't available for Alaska, Hawaii, US territories, or APO/FPO addresses; those orders ship Standard and can take 7–12 business days.

The free shipping threshold is based on the order total after discounts and before tax.

## Shipping to Canada

Canadian orders ship by tracked international standard mail and arrive in **7–14 business days**. Shipping costs CAD $14.99, or is free on orders over CAD $100. Duties and taxes are collected at checkout, so there are no extra charges on delivery.

## P.O. boxes

We ship to P.O. boxes with Standard shipping only. Oversized items, such as tents over 10 lb, can't be shipped to P.O. boxes.

## Signature required

Orders over **$500** require an adult signature on delivery. If no one is home, the carrier leaves a notice and tries again the next business day.

## Tracking

We email a tracking link when your order ships. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to update after the label is created.

## Late or lost packages

If tracking hasn't updated for **5 business days**, or your package hasn't arrived **10 business days** after the expected delivery date, contact support. We'll open a claim with the carrier and, once the package is confirmed lost, send a free replacement or a full refund, including shipping.

If tracking shows **Delivered** but you can't find your package, check with neighbors and your building's mailroom, and wait 48 hours, since carriers sometimes mark packages delivered early. If it still hasn't turned up, contact support within **14 days** of the delivery date.

## Damaged in transit

If your package arrives damaged, keep the box and packing material and take photos, then contact support within **7 days** of delivery. See the [Refund Policy](refund-policy.md) for how we handle damaged items.

## Address changes

You can change your shipping address within 1 hour of placing your order from the **Orders** page. After that, we can't change the address on the order. We aren't responsible for packages shipped to an incorrect address entered at checkout, but we'll help you contact the carrier.

## Split shipments

If your items ship from different warehouses, your order may arrive in several packages. You pay shipping only once, and each package has its own tracking number.
