# Frequently Asked Questions

Lumen & Pine is an online store for outdoor gear and home goods. This page answers the questions our support team hears most often. For full details, see the [Refund Policy](refund-policy.md) and [Shipping Policy](shipping-policy.md).

## Orders

### How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, we email you a tracking link from the carrier. You can also sign in to your account and open **Orders** to see the current status and tracking number for every order. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan after the label is created.

### Can I change or cancel my order?

You can change the shipping address or cancel an order within **1 hour** of placing it, from the **Orders** page in your account. After 1 hour the order is sent to our warehouse and can no longer be edited. If it has not shipped yet, contact support and we will try to stop it, but we can't guarantee it. If it has already shipped, you can return it under our refund policy once it arrives.

### Where is my order number?

Your order number starts with `#` and appears in the subject line of your order confirmation email (for example, "Order #1042 confirmed"). It is also listed on the **Orders** page in your account.

### I received the wrong item. What should I do?

Contact support within **7 days** of delivery with your order number and a photo of the item you received. We will send the correct item at no cost and email you a prepaid label to return the wrong one.

## Payments

### Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Lumen & Pine gift cards. We do not accept cash, checks, or cryptocurrency.

### When is my card charged?

Your card is authorized when you place the order and charged when the order ships. If an order ships in several packages, you are charged for each package as it ships.

### Do you offer price matching?

No, we don't match competitors' prices. However, if an item you bought goes on sale on our site within **14 days** of delivery, contact support and we will refund the difference once per item.

### Do you charge sales tax?

We collect sales tax for US orders where required by state law. Canadian orders are charged GST/HST/PST at checkout, so there are no extra fees on delivery.

## Account

### Do I need an account to order?

No. You can check out as a guest. With an account you can track orders, save addresses, and start returns online without contacting support.

### How do I reset my password?

Select **Sign in**, then **Forgot password**. We'll email you a reset link that is valid for 30 minutes. Our support team will never ask you for your password.

### How do I delete my account?

Email support from the address on your account and ask for account deletion. We delete your account and personal data within 30 days, except order records we're required to keep for tax purposes.

## Rewards and gift cards

### How does the Trail Rewards program work?

Members earn 1 point for every $1 spent (before tax and shipping). Every 100 points becomes a $5 reward, applied automatically at your next checkout. Points expire 12 months after they are earned. Points are removed if the order they came from is refunded.

### Do gift cards expire?

No. Lumen & Pine gift cards never expire and have no fees. Gift cards can't be exchanged for cash, except where required by law.
